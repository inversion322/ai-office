Privacy Policy: AI Office

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What this app is

AI Office is a personal automation tool that its developer (Nick G, GitHub inversion322/ai-office) runs on his own computer to prepare short videos for his own channel. It is not offered to the public: the developer is its only user.

Access requested from Google

The app asks for one YouTube permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It is used only to upload a video to the developer's own YouTube channel after the developer presses a button. The permission does not allow reading, changing or deleting existing videos, comments, subscribers or any other account data.

Data we store and share

The only thing stored is an OAuth refresh token in a local file on the developer's computer. No data is sent anywhere except to the Google and YouTube APIs. There are no analytics, cookies or advertising. We do not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/inversion322/ai-office/issues.

Политика конфиденциальности (кратко по-русски)

AI Office это личный инструмент разработчика: он запускается на его компьютере и единственный пользователь это сам разработчик. Приложение просит у Google только право youtube.upload: загрузить видео на собственный канал по нажатию кнопки. Читать, менять и удалять существующие данные канала оно не может. Хранится только токен доступа в локальном файле на компьютере разработчика. Данные никому не передаются и не продаются, аналитики и рекламы нет. Доступ можно отозвать на myaccount.google.com/permissions.
